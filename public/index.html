About Hotel Khandesh Darbar

Experience Authentic Khandeshi Food in Sangli

Welcome to Hotel Khandesh Darbar, the premier pure vegetarian restaurant destination in Sangli. We take immense pride in crafting authentic Maharashtrian & Khandeshi meals using age-old recipes, pure cow ghee, fresh farm vegetables, and signature homemade spices.

Whether dining with family, celebrating with friends, or enjoying a quick satisfying lunch, our spotless hygiene, comfortable AC dining hall, and warm Maharashtrian hospitality promise a memorable experience.
