About Us

About Hotel Khandesh Darbar

Founded on 11 November 2025, Hotel Khandesh Darbar was created with a simple vision—to serve authentic Khandeshi and Maharashtrian cuisine made with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes.

Every dish is prepared with care to deliver the original taste of Khandesh while maintaining the highest standards of quality, hygiene, and hospitality.

Whether you're visiting with family, friends, or colleagues, we welcome you to enjoy a memorable dining experience.
